## Week 3-4: Optimize & Scale

### Improve Your AI Workflow

**My efficient workflow:**

**1. Research (10 min)**
- Perplexity AI: Find top 5 competitor articles
- Note what they cover
- Find gaps/unique angles

**2. Outline (5 min)**
ChatGPT prompt:
```
"Create a detailed outline for '[Title]'.
Research shows top articles cover [topics].
Include these sections plus [unique angle].
Target word count: [number]."
```

**3. First Draft (15 min)**
ChatGPT prompt:
```
"Write section 1 of the outline: [section title].
Include: 2-3 examples, data/stats, actionable tips.
Tone: [conversational/professional].
```

**4. Edit (25 min)**
- Remove AI clichés

- Insert client-specific examples
- Improve hooks and transitions
- Add formatting (bullets, bold, headers)

**5. Proofread (5 min)**

- Read aloud (catches awkward phrasing)
- Check facts

**Total time per 1,000-word article:** 60 minutes

## The AI Writing Process (Detailed)

### Step 1: Research (10 min)

**Tools:** Perplexity AI, Google

**What to find:**
- Top 5 competitor articles
- Key points they cover
- What's missing
- Recent stats/data

**Prompt:**
```
"What are the main topics covered in articles
about [topic]? Summarize the top 5 search results."
```

### Step 2: Outline (5 min)

**ChatGPT prompt:**
```
"Create a blog outline for '[Title]'.
Target audience: [description]
Competitor articles cover: [topics from research]
Unique angle: [your twist]
Structure: Intro, 5-7 main sections, conclusion
Include: examples, stats, actionable tips"
```

**Review and adjust:**
- Reorder sections for better flow
- Add client-specific requirements
- Note where to add examples

### Step 3: Generate Sections (15 min)

**Write each section separately:**

```
"Write the introduction for this outline:
[paste outline]

Include:
- Hook (surprising stat or question)
- Why this matters to [audience]
- What they'll learn
- Keep it 150-200 words
- Conversational tone"
```

**Then for each section:**
```
"Write section 3: [section title]

Based on this outline: [paste relevant section]

Include:
- 2-3 specific examples
- Data or statistics
- Actionable takeaway
- 200-250 words"
```

**Why sections, not full article?**
- Better quality per section
- More control
- Easier to edit
- Less repetition

### Step 4: Edit (25 min)

**Look for AI tells:**
- "delve," "landscape," "realm," "unlock"
- "It's worth noting that..."
- "In today's digital landscape..."
- Overly formal language

**Add personality:**
- Replace generic examples with specific ones
- Add client's brand voice
- Insert "you" and "I" language
- Break up long paragraphs

**Improve readability:**
- Shorter sentences
- Bullet points
- Bold key phrases
- Clear headers

### Step 5: Proofread (5 min)

**Grammarly check:**
- Fix grammar errors
- Reduce passive voice
- Check tone

**Read aloud:**
- Catches awkward phrasing
- Ensures natural flow

**Final checklist:**
- Meets word count
- Includes all required elements
- Has clear CTA
- No obvious AI language

## FAQ

**Q: Do I need to tell clients I use AI?**
A: Most contracts say "deliver quality content." They don't care how. If asked directly, say "I use AI tools for research and drafting, but I personally write and edit everything."

**Q: Can clients detect AI content?**
A: Raw ChatGPT output, yes. Properly edited content (30%+ rewrite), no.

**Q: What if I'm not a great writer?**
A: You need to be 6/10. AI gets you to 8/10 with editing.

**Q: How long to $2K/month?**
A: 8-12 weeks if you work 15-20 hours/week.

**Q: Best niche for beginners?**
A: SaaS, real estate, or e-commerce. High demand, good pay.
