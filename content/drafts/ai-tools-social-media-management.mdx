## My Complete AI Social Media Stack

### For Content Creation

#### 1. ChatGPT Plus ($20/month)

**What I use it for:**
- Caption writing (80% of captions)
- Content calendar ideas
- Hashtag research
- Engagement responses
- Bio writing

**Best prompts:**

**Caption batch creation:**
```
"Create 10 Instagram captions for a [industry] business.

Audience: [description]
Tone: [casual/professional/inspirational]
Topics: [list 3-4 themes]

For each caption:
- 100-150 words
- Include emoji (moderate use)
- Add question or CTA
- 5 relevant hashtags
- Make them varied (don't sound repetitive)"
```

**Content calendar:**
```
"Create a 30-day Instagram content calendar for [business type].

Audience: [target]
Goals: [engagement/sales/education]

Mix:
- 40% educational
- 30% entertaining
- 20% promotional
- 10% behind-the-scenes

For each day provide:
- Topic
- Content type (carousel/reel/single image)
- Brief description
- Suggested hashtags"
```

**Engagement replies:**
```
"Write 5 friendly replies to these Instagram comments:

1. [Comment 1]
2. [Comment 2]
3. [Comment 3]

Keep them:
- 1-2 sentences
- Warm and genuine
- Continue conversation
- Brand voice: [description]"
```

**Pros:**
- ✅ Best quality AI writing
- ✅ Understands context well
- ✅ Consistent brand voice
- ✅ Can do long-form captions

**Cons:**
- ❌ $20/month (but worth it)
- ❌ Sometimes too formal
- ❌ Need to edit for personality

**Time saved:** 5-6 hours per client/month

**ROI:** Pay $20, earn extra $1,000+/month from efficiency

**Pros:**

### For Scheduling & Management

#### 5. Buffer (Free for 3 channels, $6/month for 8)

**What I use it for:**
- Scheduling posts
- Managing multiple accounts
- Analytics
- Team collaboration

**Why Buffer over others:**
- Clean interface
- Reliable posting
- Good free tier
- Simple pricing

**Free tier:**
- 3 social channels
- 10 scheduled posts per channel
- Basic analytics

**Essentials ($6/month):**
- 8 social channels
- Unlimited scheduled posts
- Better analytics
- Landing page builder

**My workflow:**
1. Create content in Canva/ChatGPT
2. Upload to Buffer
3. Schedule for optimal times
4. Review analytics weekly

**Pros:**
- ✅ Simple and reliable
- ✅ Affordable
- ✅ Great free tier
- ✅ Browser extension

**Cons:**
- ❌ Analytics less detailed than Hootsuite
- ❌ No Instagram Stories scheduling (free)
- ❌ Limited AI features

**Alternatives:**
- **Later** (Free for 30 posts/month): Better for Instagram
- **Hootsuite** ($99/month): Enterprise features, overkill for freelancers
- **Meta Business Suite** (Free): Only for Facebook/Instagram

**My recommendation:** Start with Buffer free. Upgrade to $6/month when you have 4+ clients.

### For Video Content

#### 7. Descript ($12/month for Creator plan)

**What I use it for:**
- Editing Reels/TikToks
- Auto-captions
- Removing filler words
- Screen recordings

**Key features:**
- Edit video like text document
- AI removes "ums" and pauses
- Auto-generates captions
- Overdub (AI voice cloning)

**Workflow:**
1. Client sends raw video
2. Upload to Descript
3. AI removes filler words
4. Add captions automatically
5. Export for Instagram/TikTok

**Time saved:** 30 minutes per video → 5 minutes

**Pros:**
- ✅ Easiest video editing
- ✅ Auto-captions are accurate
- ✅ Overdub feature is incredible

**Cons:**
- ❌ $12/month (but worth it if you do video)
- ❌ Limited to 10 hours/month transcription
- ❌ Learning curve

**Worth it if:** You edit 5+ videos/month for clients.

**Alternative:** CapCut (Free, but no AI features)

#### 8. OpusClip ($9.50/month for Starter)

**What I use it for:**
- Turning long videos into short clips
- AI identifies best moments
- Auto-generates captions
- Creates viral-style clips

**Use case:**
Client has 20-minute YouTube video → OpusClip creates 10 short clips for TikTok/Reels → Post 10 days of content from 1 video.

**Pros:**
- ✅ Saves hours of video editing
- ✅ AI finds best moments
- ✅ Auto-captions included
- ✅ Viral score prediction

**Cons:**
- ❌ AI sometimes picks wrong moments
- ❌ Need to review all clips
- ❌ Works best with talking-head videos

**Worth it if:** You repurpose long-form content into shorts.

## Common Mistakes (I Made These)

### 1. Creating Content Manually
**Problem:** Spent 6 hours creating 30 posts
**Solution:** Batch with ChatGPT (90 minutes for 30 posts)

### 2. Scheduling Daily
**Problem:** Wasted 10-15 min/day scheduling
**Solution:** Schedule whole month at once (30 min)

### 3. Not Using Templates
**Problem:** Designing from scratch (30 min per graphic)
**Solution:** Saved templates in Canva (5 min per graphic)

### 4. Over-delivering
**Problem:** Spent 8 hours on $300/month client
**Solution:** Set clear scope, stick to it

### 5. Manual Analytics
**Problem:** Screenshot everything, make manual reports (2 hours)
**Solution:** Use Metricool auto-reports (10 minutes)
