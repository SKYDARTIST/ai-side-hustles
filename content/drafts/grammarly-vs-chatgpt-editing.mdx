# Grammarly vs ChatGPT for Editing

I edited 120 articles with both tools. Here's what actually works.

## The Quick Verdict

**For grammar & typos:** Grammarly wins
**For rewriting & flow:** ChatGPT wins
**Best approach:** Use both (here's how)

## What I Tested

**Test period:** 2 months (Dec 2025 - Jan 2026)

**Content:**
- 120 articles (1,000-1,500 words each)
- Mix of AI-written and human-written
- Blog posts, emails, social media

**Criteria:**
- Grammar accuracy
- Readability improvement
- Time to edit
- Cost effectiveness
- Final quality

## Grammarly: The Breakdown

### What I Tested
- **Free version:** 40 articles
- **Premium version:** 40 articles ($12/month)

### What Grammarly Does Well

#### 1. Grammar & Spelling (Excellent)
**Catches:**
- Typos and misspellings (99% accuracy)
- Grammar errors
- Punctuation mistakes
- Subject-verb agreement
- Comma splices

**Example:**
- ❌ "Your going to love this product"
- ✅ "You're going to love this product"

**Verdict:** Best-in-class for technical errors.

#### 2. Clarity Suggestions (Very Good)
**Premium feature:**
- Identifies wordy sentences
- Suggests concise alternatives
- Flags passive voice
- Points out unclear references

**Example:**
- ❌ "The report was completed by the team yesterday"
- ✅ "The team completed the report yesterday"

**Verdict:** Helpful, but sometimes over-corrects.

#### 3. Tone Detection (Good)
**Premium feature:**
- Detects if you sound formal/casual
- Suggests tone adjustments
- Confidence level indicator

**Reality:** Works okay but not essential.

#### 4. Plagiarism Detection (Excellent)
**Premium feature:**
- Checks against billions of web pages
- Essential for client work
- 100% accurate in my tests

**Verdict:** Worth it if you write for clients.

### What Grammarly Struggles With

#### 1. Context Understanding
**Problem:** Doesn't understand nuance

**Example:**
- Text: "This is sick" (meaning cool)
- Grammarly: Suggests "This is ill" 🤦

#### 2. Creative Writing
**Problem:** Pushes formal, corporate tone

**My experience:**
- Tried to make casual blog posts too formal
- Removed personality
- Flagged intentional fragments

#### 3. AI-Written Content Detection
**Problem:** Doesn't catch AI patterns

**What it misses:**
- Repetitive phrasing
- AI clichés ("delve," "landscape")
- Unnatural flow

**ChatGPT is better for this.**

#### 4. Rewriting Suggestions
**Problem:** Basic rephasing

**Example:**
- Original: "The product is really good and customers like it a lot"
- Grammarly: "The product is excellent, and customers appreciate it"
- Better (ChatGPT): "Customers love this product"

### Grammarly Pricing

**Free:**
- Grammar & spelling
- Basic suggestions
- 100% fine for personal use

**Premium ($12/month):**
- Clarity suggestions
- Tone detection
- Plagiarism checker
- Word choice
- Formality level

**Business ($15/user/month):**
- Style guide
- Team features
- Brand tones
- Analytics

**Worth it?**
- Free: Yes, always use it
- Premium: Yes if you write for clients
- Business: Only if you have a team

## ChatGPT: The Breakdown

### What I Tested
- **GPT-4 (Plus, $20/month):** 60 articles
- **Free tier:** 20 articles

### What ChatGPT Does Well

#### 1. Rewrites for Clarity (Excellent)
**What it does:**
- Makes complex sentences simple
- Improves flow
- Fixes awkward phrasing
- Restructures paragraphs

**Example prompt:**
```
"Make this clearer and more concise:

[paste text]

Keep the same message but improve flow."
```

**Result:** Usually 30-40% better readability.

#### 2. Removing AI "Tells" (Excellent)
**What it catches:**
- Repetitive phrases
- AI clichés
- Unnatural transitions
- Robotic tone

**Example:**
- ❌ "In today's digital landscape, it's important to delve into..."
- ✅ "You need to understand..."

**Prompt:**
```
"Rewrite this to sound more human and less AI-generated:

[paste text]

Remove phrases like 'delve,' 'landscape,' 'it's worth noting.'"
```

**Verdict:** Essential for AI-written content.

#### 3. Tone Adjustments (Excellent)
**What it does:**
- Shifts from formal to casual
- Adds or removes personality
- Matches brand voice

**Example prompt:**
```
"Rewrite this in a more conversational, friendly tone:

[paste text]

Add personality but keep it professional."
```

**Verdict:** Way better than Grammarly's tone feature.

#### 4. Structural Edits (Very Good)
**What it does:**
- Reorganizes content for better flow
- Suggests section improvements
- Fixes transitions

**Example prompt:**
```
"This section feels disjointed. Improve the flow:

[paste section]"
```

### What ChatGPT Struggles With

#### 1. Grammar Accuracy (Good, Not Great)
**Problem:** Misses subtle errors

**Example missed:**
- "Their going to the store" (should be "They're")
- Sometimes caught, sometimes not

**Verdict:** Use Grammarly for grammar, ChatGPT for everything else.

#### 2. Consistency
**Problem:** Sometimes changes too much

**Example:**
- Asked to make text "slightly more casual"
- ChatGPT rewrote 80% of it

**Solution:** Be very specific in prompts.

#### 3. Fact-Checking
**Problem:** Doesn't verify facts

**Example:**
- Original: "This happened in 2023"
- ChatGPT rewrites: "This happened recently"
- Loses specificity

**Solution:** Always verify facts yourself.

#### 4. Over-Simplification
**Problem:** Sometimes dumbs down content

**Example:**
- Technical content → too simple
- Nuance lost

**Solution:** Specify reading level in prompt.

**What it does:**

**Pros:** Good paraphrasing

**When to use:** Rewriting small sections

**What it does:**
- Similar to Grammarly
- More detailed reports
- Style checking

**Pros:** One-time purchase option
**Cons:** Slower, clunkier interface

**When to use:** If you want Grammarly alternative
