# Midjourney vs DALL-E 3: Which Makes You More Money?

I've spent 6 months using both. Here's what actually matters for making money.

## The Quick Answer

**For most freelancers:** Midjourney wins on quality, DALL-E 3 wins on speed and cost.

**For serious income:** You'll probably end up using both.

## Pricing Breakdown

### Midjourney
- **Basic Plan:** $10/month (200 images)
- **Standard Plan:** $30/month (unlimited relaxed mode)
- **Pro Plan:** $60/month (unlimited fast mode)

**What you actually need:** Standard plan ($30/month) for client work.

### DALL-E 3
- **Via ChatGPT Plus:** $20/month (included with Plus)
- **Via API:** Pay-per-image ($0.04-0.08 per image)
- **Free tier:** None for DALL-E 3

**What you actually need:** ChatGPT Plus ($20/month) is enough to start.

## Common Questions

**Q: Can I use free alternatives?**
A: Yes (Stable Diffusion, Bing Image Creator), but clients can tell the quality difference. Hard to charge premium rates.

**Q: Which one do clients prefer?**
A: They don't care about the tool. They care about results. Midjourney results look more "professional" to most clients.

**Q: Can I make $2K/month with just one?**
A: Yes. I know people doing $3K/month with just Midjourney. And others doing $2K+ with just DALL-E 3. Tool matters less than marketing.

**Q: Do I need to tell clients which AI I used?**
A: Check your contract. Most clients don't care as long as they get commercial rights. Be transparent if asked.

**Q: What about image editing?**
A: Budget for Photoshop ($10/month) or use free tools (Photopea, GIMP). You'll need to edit 30-50% of AI images.
