# Best AI Tools Under $10 per Month

There is a common misconception that you need to spend $100 a month on subscriptions to be "serious" about AI. For many side hustlers, spending $20 on ChatGPT Plus, $20 on Claude Pro, and $30 on Midjourney just isn't realistic in the beginning.

I have spent the last few months testing "budget" AI platforms. Most under-$10 tools are useless, but there are a few diamonds that offer incredible value for the price of a couple of coffees.

## 1. Notion AI ($10/month)
If you already use Notion to organize your life or your business, their built-in AI assistant is a steal at $10. It can summarize long research notes, brainstorm project ideas, and rewrite your text directly inside your workspace.

**The Reality Check**: It’s not as "smart" as the full version of GPT-4 for complex logic, but for organizational tasks, it’s much faster because you never have to leave your notes.

## 2. Canva Pro ($9.99/month on sale, normally slightly more)
While the free version is great, the Pro version is the best $10-ish investment a freelancer can make. The "Background Remover" and "Magic Resize" tools alone will save you hours of manual work every month.

**Explanation**: "Magic Resize" is an AI feature that takes one design (like an Instagram post) and automatically creates copies of it for other platforms like LinkedIn, Pinterest, and Facebook Stories. It handles the layout adjustments for you so you don't have to start from scratch for every size.

## 3. Poe ($9.99/month for mobile)
Poe is an aggregator. Instead of paying for five different AI models separately, Poe gives you access to GPT-4, Claude 3.5, and Gemini all in one interface.

**The Reality Check**: You have a "compute points" limit, meaning you can't send infinite messages to the most expensive models. But for most freelancers, the $10 tier provides more than enough daily access.

## 4. QuillBot Premium ($9.95/month)
If your side hustle involve writing, QuillBot is an excellent companion. The premium version allows you to paraphrase unlimited text and includes a plagiarism checker.

**Explanation**: A Plagiarism Checker is a tool that compares your writing against billions of web pages to ensure it's original. This is vital when working with AI, as it gives your clients peace of mind that the content won't be flagged for copying other sources.

## 5. Otter.ai (Budget Tier)
If you handle many client calls or podcasts, Otter's transcription is highly accurate and very affordable on their basic paid tier.

**Real-world use**: I use it to transcribe my client discovery calls. I can focus 100% on the conversation, and then look at the AI summary afterward to pull out the client's specific requirements.

## When to Stay Budget and When to Upgrade

You should stick to these under-$10 tools until your side hustle is earning at least $500 in profit every month. 

**My Rule of Thumb**: Your "Tool Budget" should never exceed 5-10% of your total revenue. If you're making $200 a month, spend $10. If you're making $2,000, that’s when it makes sense to pay for the "big" $20-$30 subscriptions.

The goal isn't to have the most tools; it’s to have the highest ROI (Return on Investment) on the tools you do have.

> [!TIP]
> **The Hidden Budget Insight**: I've found that using the **Poe ($10)** aggregator is actually smarter than paying for **Claude ($20)** if you're a low-volume writer. You get 90% of the same power for 50% of the cost. Always audit your usage before you auto-renew a $20 sub.
