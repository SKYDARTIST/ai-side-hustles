# How to Use Claude for Content Writing

While ChatGPT gets most of the headlines, many professional writers have quietly switched to Claude for their heavy lifting. Why? Because Claude’s writing style is inherently more natural and less "robotic" than its competitors. It follows complex instructions more accurately and has a much larger "memory" for long projects.

I use Claude for 90% of my client articles. Here is my complete system for turning a blank page into a finished 1,500-word post in about an hour.

## Why Claude Wins at Writing

1.  **More Human Voice**: Claude tends to use a wider variety of sentence structures and avoids the overly cheerful, "marketing" tone that ChatGPT often defaults to.
2.  **Larger Context Window**: You can upload multiple PDFs, research papers, or past articles, and Claude will remember all of them while writing.
3.  **Better Instruction Following**: If you tell Claude "don't use the word 'delve'," it actually listens.

**Explanation**: A "Context Window" is essentially the AI's short-term memory. A larger window allows the AI to process more information (like a 50-page report) at once without forgetting the beginning by the time it reaches the end.

## The 4-Step Writing Process

### 1. The Strategy Phase
Don't just ask Claude to "write a blog post." Start by giving it your research and asking it to find the most interesting angles. 
**Prompt**: "I am writing for [Audience]. Based on these three research links, what are the three most controversial or surprising points I should cover?"

### 2. The Structured Outline
Once you have an angle, ask for a detailed outline. I specify exactly where I want headers, bullet points, and real-world examples.

### 3. Modular Drafting
**The secret to high quality**: Write one section at a time. 
Ask Claude: "Write the introduction for the outline above. Make it punchy, use the first person, and include a surprising statistic." 
By doing it section-by-section, you maintain much more control over the quality.

### 4. The "Human" Pass
Even Claude needs a final polish. I look for any remaining AI clichés and replace them with my own slang or industry-specific terms.

---

## The Reality Check
Claude is an assistant, not a ghostwriter. If you don't provide the research and the unique perspective, the output will still be generic. Your job is to be the "Director"—you provide the vision, and Claude handles the execution.

---

## Final Recommendation
If you are doing high-volume, low-cost work, ChatGPT is fine. If you are charging $150+ per article for premium clients, you should be using Claude. The difference in quality is noticeable to clients.
