# Grammarly vs ChatGPT for Editing

If you are a freelance writer in 2026, you likely use both of these tools. But many people use them incorrectly, trying to make one tool do a job it wasn't built for. I have edited over 100 articles using both, and the difference is clear: one is for **Accuracy**, and the other is for **Style**.

## Grammarly: The Technical Guardian

Grammarly is still the king of technical correctness. If you have a typo, a misplaced comma, or an awkward subject-verb agreement error, Grammarly will catch it 99% of the time.

- **Best for**: Final proofreading, catching typos, and plagiarism checks.
- **The Reality Check**: Grammarly can be a bit "stiff." It often suggests removing intentional sentence fragments or casual phrasing that actually makes your writing more engaging.

**Explanation**: A "Plagiarism Check" is a scan that compares your work against billions of web pages to ensure it isn't too similar to existing content. For a professional writer, this is an essential insurance policy.

## ChatGPT: The Stylistic Editor

While Grammarly focuses on the rules, ChatGPT focuses on the flow. It is significantly better at "re-writing" a clunky paragraph to make it more readable.

- **Best for**: Changing the tone of a piece (e.g., from formal to casual) and improving the overall narrative flow.
- **The Reality Check**: ChatGPT is not a reliable proofreader. It can sometimes "hallucinate" grammar fixes or unintentionally change the meaning of a sentence while trying to make it sound better.

## My Professional Workflow

I never pick one over the other. I use them in a specific order:

1.  **Draft with AI Assistance**: Use Claude or ChatGPT to get my ideas down.
2.  **The Style Pass (ChatGPT)**: I ask ChatGPT to "Read this and improve the flow while keeping my original voice."
3.  **The Accuracy Pass (Grammarly)**: I paste the result into Grammarly to catch the technical errors that ChatGPT missed.
4.  **The Human Read-Aloud**: I read the final version out loud to myself. If a sentence makes me stumble, I rewrite it manually.

---

## The Bottom Line

If you can only afford one paid tool, get **ChatGPT Plus**. The free version of Grammarly is usually enough to catch basic typos, but the "smart" editing of ChatGPT Plus will save you far more time in the long run.
