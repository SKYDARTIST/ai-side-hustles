Remember 2016?

If you were in India, you remember the chaos. Before September 2016, we were paying ₹200-300 for a measly 1GB of data. We tracked every megabyte like it was liquid gold. Video calls? Forget it. Streaming? Only if you were rich.

Then Mukesh Ambani walked on stage and changed everything.

Reliance Jio launched with a promise that sounded insane: **free voice calls for life** and data at throwaway prices. The industry laughed. Airtel, Vodafone, Idea—they all thought Jio would burn cash and disappear.

Instead, [India went from having the world's most expensive data to the cheapest](https://restofworld.org/2020/how-india-mobile-data-became-worlds-cheapest/). Prices fell 95%. Data consumption jumped from under 1GB per month to over 10GB. Internet users exploded from 300 million to over 700 million by 2020.

Jio didn't just disrupt telecom. It **commoditized connectivity**.

And in 2026, history is repeating itself.

Except this time, it's not data that's becoming a commodity.

**It's intelligence.**

---

## The 98% Cost Collapse Nobody's Talking About

In 2023, running GPT-4 level reasoning cost you [$30-60 per million tokens](https://llm-stats.com/ai-trends). That was expensive. Every API call mattered. Startups counted tokens like early Jio users counted megabytes.

Fast forward to February 2026.

Here's what the same intelligence costs now:

**Budget Tier:**
- [DeepSeek V3.2](https://pricepertoken.com/pricing-page/provider/deepseek): $0.27/$1.10 per million tokens
- [Gemini Flash-Lite](https://cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/generative-ai/pricing): $0.10 per million tokens
- [DeepSeek R1](https://deepseek.ai/pricing) (with reasoning!): $0.55/$2.19 per million tokens

**Mid Tier:**
- [Claude Sonnet 4.5](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/pricing): $3/$15 per million tokens
- Gemini Pro: $3-15 per million tokens

**Premium Tier:**
- [Claude Opus 4.6](https://www.aifreeapi.com/en/posts/claude-opus-4-pricing): $5/$25 per million tokens
- [GPT-5](https://pricepertoken.com/pricing-page/model/openai-gpt-5): $1.25/$10 per million tokens
- [GPT-5.2](https://pricepertoken.com/pricing-page/model/openai-gpt-5.2): $1.75/$14 per million tokens

Let me put that in perspective.

A complex task that cost you $15 with GPT-4 in 2023 now costs **$0.50** with DeepSeek V3.2.

That's a **97% reduction** in just three years.

According to [Epoch AI's analysis](https://epoch.ai/data-insights/llm-inference-price-trends), inference prices for LLMs have fallen between **9x to 900x per year** depending on the model tier. The median? **50x per year**. And when they excluded data before January 2024, that median jumped to **200x per year**.

Read that again: 200x annual price reduction.

## The Jio Pattern: Cheaper Prices, Higher Usage

Here's where it gets interesting.

When Jio slashed data prices in 2016, something counterintuitive happened.

People didn't just use the same amount of data for less money.

They used **10-100x more data**.

Before Jio: Under 1GB per month.
After Jio: Over 10GB per month.

Why? Because when something becomes cheap enough, behavior changes entirely. Video streaming exploded. UPI payments went mainstream. Entire startups were built on assumptions of cheap data.

**The exact same thing is happening with AI tokens right now.**

Modern reasoning models like GPT-5 with o3, Claude Opus 4.6, and DeepSeek R1 don't just run queries. They "think" internally, burning through tokens to solve complex problems.

So while the price per token dropped 98%, the **tokens per task** increased 5-10x for reasoning workloads.

Your total bill might stay flat.

But the **intelligence per dollar** just 10x'd.

According to [Stanford's 2025 AI Index Report](https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index/2025-ai-index-report), enterprises are now processing workloads that were economically impossible just 18 months ago.

## The Open-Source Shockwave

Remember what killed the old telecom oligopoly?

It wasn't just Jio's pricing. It was that **data became infrastructure**.

Once everyone had cheap data, the competitive advantage shifted elsewhere—to apps, to services, to distribution.

The same shift is happening in AI.

Open-source models forced this price war.

- **Llama 4** (Scout / Maverick)
- **DeepSeek V3.2** and **R1**
- **Mistral** family

These models proved that [open-source alternatives are approximately 90% cheaper](https://www.amadeuscapital.com/ai-commoditisation-curve/) than closed-source models at the same intelligence level.

Closed labs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google) can't charge "intelligence premium" anymore.

They now compete on:
- Latency (speed)
- Context windows (how much they can remember)
- Tooling and ecosystem integration
- Safety and reliability
- Enterprise support

Sound familiar? That's exactly what telecom became after Jio—a race for network quality, customer service, and bundled apps.

## The Real Numbers: Disruption By the Data

Let's look at what actually happened to the telecom market after Jio launched.

**Before Jio (2016):**
- Data cost: ₹200-300 per 1GB
- Average revenue per user (ARPU): ₹200
- Market leaders: Airtel, Vodafone, Idea

**After Jio (2018-2020):**
- Data cost: [95% cheaper](https://arthnova.com/jio-disrupted-india-telecom-free-data/)
- ARPU: Dropped 40% to ₹120
- Market casualties: Reliance Communications (bankrupt), Tata Teleservices (shut down), Telenor (exited), MTS (exited)
- [Jio grabbed 100 million subscribers](https://thekarostartup.com/reliance-jio-revolution/) in record time—the fastest growth any telecom company achieved globally

Now compare that to AI in 2026:

**Before Open-Source Disruption (2023):**
- GPT-4 level intelligence: $30-60 per million tokens
- Market leaders: OpenAI, Anthropic, Google
- Open-source: <5% market share

**After Open-Source Disruption (2026):**
- [Same intelligence: $0.27-5 per million tokens](https://pricepertoken.com/)
- Price reduction: 90-98%
- Open-source market share: [13-30% depending on use case](https://www.amadeuscapital.com/ai-commoditisation-curve/)
- Enterprise cost savings: [100x reduction for equivalent workloads](https://medium.com/@jsmith0475/the-100x-cost-reduction-reshaping-enterprise-ai-0e2779fca872)

The pattern is identical.

## What This Means If You're Building with AI

Here's the brutal truth:

If you're still paying 2024 prices in 2026, you're on a pre-Jio plan.

**The new playbook:**

### 1. Use "Flash/Mini" models for 90% of tasks

Most of your workload doesn't need GPT-5 Opus-level intelligence.

- Customer support? Gemini Flash at $0.10 per million.
- Content summarization? DeepSeek V3.2 at $0.27 per million.
- Simple classification? Claude Haiku at $1 per million.

Save the heavy hitters for what actually matters.

### 2. Leverage cost-saving features

- **Batch processing**: [50% off on Claude models](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/pricing)
- **Prompt caching**: [Up to 90% savings](https://www.aifreeapi.com/en/posts/claude-opus-4-pricing) on repeated context
- **Streaming responses**: Lower latency, better UX

### 3. The moat is NOT the model anymore

Just like you don't build a business around selling electricity, you can't build a sustainable business around access to LLMs.

The moat is:
- **Distribution**: Can you reach users?
- **Data pipelines**: Do you have proprietary data?
- **Workflow integration**: Are you embedded in daily habits?
- **Trust and reliability**: Do users trust you with sensitive tasks?

## The Uncomfortable Questions

But here's what keeps me up at night.

Is this full commoditization?

Or are we watching a temporary price war before consolidation?

Look at what happened with telecom. After the bloodbath, India went from 12 major operators to effectively 3 (Jio, Airtel, Vi). The survivors raised prices once the competition died.

Will AI follow the same path?

**Arguments for commoditization:**
- Open-source is real and improving faster than closed models
- Hardware costs dropping 30% annually
- Inference optimization getting 40% more efficient each year
- No single player can maintain a moat through model quality alone

**Arguments against:**
- [OpenAI still controls 70-87% of enterprise market share](https://www.amadeuscapital.com/ai-commoditisation-curve/)
- Enterprises pay premium for reliability and trust
- Context windows and latency still differentiate
- Safety and alignment remain unsolved at commodity pricing

I don't know the answer.

But I do know this: **the window to build with cheap intelligence is RIGHT NOW**.

In 2016, Jio didn't just make data cheaper.

It **changed what was possible** with that data.

Suddenly, UPI payments worked in rural villages. Food delivery became viable in tier-2 cities. EdTech reached students who never had reliable internet.

The infrastructure got so cheap, you could build entire businesses on assumptions of zero data cost.

That's **transformation**.

It's already here.
