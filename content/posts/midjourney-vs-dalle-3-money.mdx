# Midjourney vs DALL-E 3: Quality vs. Convenience

If you want to make money with AI-generated images, you need to understand the difference between these two powerhouses. They both create stunning visuals, but they are built for very different workflows.

I use both in my daily work. Here is how I decide which one to use for each project.

## Midjourney: The Professional Artist’s Choice

Midjourney is widely considered to produce the highest quality images in the AI space. It handles lighting, texture, and complex artistic styles better than anything else.

- **Best for**: Book covers, high-end branding, and detailed illustrations.
- **The Reality Check**: It lives inside Discord, which makes the user interface feel clunky and disorganized. There is also a steeper learning curve to master the "parameters" needed for perfect results.

**Explanation**: "Parameters" are short pieces of code (like `--ar 16:9` or `--v 6`) that you add to the end of a prompt to tell the AI exactly how to render the image. Learning these is essential for professional-level control.

## DALL-E 3: The King of Convenience

DALL-E 3 is built directly into ChatGPT. This makes it incredibly easy to use because you can just "talk" to it to make changes.

- **Best for**: Social media graphics, blog featured images, and quick mockups.
- **Strengths**: It is much better than Midjourney at following complex instructions and adding actual text to images.
- **The Reality Check**: The images can sometimes look a bit "plastic" or overly digital compared to the rich, cinematic quality of Midjourney.

## Which One Makes More Money?

In my experience, you can charge higher rates for Midjourney work. When a client sees a Midjourney-generated book cover, they often can't tell it was made by AI. DALL-E images sometimes have a "tell" that clients might associate with lower-cost work.

However, DALL-E is much faster. If you are managing 10 social media clients, the speed of DALL-E might allow you to complete more work and earn more profit overall, even at a lower per-project rate.

---

## Final Recommendation
Start with **DALL-E 3** because it’s included in your ChatGPT Plus subscription. Once you land a client who needs "premium" visuals (like a logo or a website hero image), that is the time to spend $30 on a Midjourney subscription.
